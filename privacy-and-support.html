Last Round

Party games for adults. Support and privacy information.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email FisherEntertainment@outlook.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the All-Nighter screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. This works on a new phone too, as long as you use the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel the weekly plan?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Last Round, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least a day before the next renewal.

Playing responsibly

Last Round is for adults 18 and older. A penalty can be anything your group agrees on, such as a forfeit, a dare or a non-alcoholic drink. Anyone can sit out a card. Please never drive after drinking.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The short version: Last Round has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

Player names

Names you type in as players are used only while the app is open. They are not saved and not sent anywhere. They are cleared when you close the app.

Purchases

Payments for the weekly plan and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check and restore what you have bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that processes purchase and subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase information. Its privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your IP address and basic device information. See Google's privacy policy.

Information we collect

We do not run analytics in the app and do not collect personal information ourselves. We do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Last Round contains adult themes and is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to delete. If you want purchase records handled by Apple or RevenueCat, contact them directly, or email us and we will point you in the right direction.
